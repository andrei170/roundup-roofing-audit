All Three Of Them Are Running A Meta Pixel. You Are Running Nothing.

3 / 3
Dallas competitors tracking visitors. You: none.

Every firm we checked can advertise to someone who visited their site last week. You cannot reach a single past visitor.

In a hail market that matters more than anywhere else. When a storm hits, they already know who has been looking at roofs. You start each time from a cold door.

The Row That Matters Most
Elevated Roofing is carrying a Google Ads conversion tag

That tag only exists on a site that is spending money on Google and measuring what comes back. They are buying the searches you are hoping to be found for, and they can tell you exactly what each booked job cost them.

Arrington publishes 258 pages and has been trading since 1983. Bert since 1988, with over a hundred reviews on the page. These are not firms you beat by working harder. They are firms you beat by picking the ground they are not standing on.

Where You Can Win
Four things that are true of you and not of them
  • You have a door-knocker who is producing. None of these firms has that, and it is the one channel that cannot be bought with a bigger ad budget. Pair it with tracking and you know which streets to send him down.
  • You are small enough to answer the phone. A homeowner who rings a firm with 1,000+ reviews is a ticket in a queue. With you they get the owner.
  • The suburbs are wide open. You already name ten cities. Arrington covers the metro with 258 pages; Bert publishes almost nothing. There is room in Frisco, Garland, Mesquite and Denton for somebody who turns up properly.
  • Storm response rewards speed, not size. A small firm that can be in front of a hit neighbourhood in 48 hours beats a big one that takes a week to mobilise — but only if the pixel is already running before the storm, not after.
What We Could Not Check
Marked honestly rather than guessed
  • Two Dallas firms we tried did not respond to our check at all and are not in the table.
  • Anyone's revenue, job volume, crew size, pricing, close rate or ad spend, including yours. A tag proves somebody is measuring, not that they are winning.
  • Whether Arrington or Bert advertise — no Google Ads tag was found on either, which is not proof they do not advertise.
  • Your own Google review count and rating. Your site shows a rating but no count, and we did not verify it against Google.
  • Every roofing firm in DFW. This is the set we could find and verify in the time available, not a complete census, and it excludes the national storm-chasers who work this market after events.

They are measuring. You are guessing. That is the entire gap.

Not budget, not crew, not years. Instruments.
